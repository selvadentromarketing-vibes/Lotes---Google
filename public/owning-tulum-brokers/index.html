The evening

What your clients will learn

Two hours of clear answers for anyone thinking about owning in Tulum, whether to live, to rent out, or simply to hold.

How a foreigner buys in Tulum

Tulum sits in Mexico’s restricted coastal zone, so foreign buyers hold title through a fideicomiso, a bank trust. What it is, how it is set up, and how ownership is held and passed on.

The real costs

What it takes beyond the price: closing and notary fees, setting up and keeping the trust, property tax and maintenance, so they budget for the whole picture.

Different ways to invest

Land to build on, a finished residence, or ownership by the week. How each model earns, what each one asks of the owner, and who it suits.
